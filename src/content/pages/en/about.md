## About me

A developer working on databases and systems, based in Hefei, China. Mostly C++ and Rust, with an interest in storage engines, query execution, and the training and inference stacks behind language models.

A few things I've worked on:

- **[CSCC-DB / Rucbase 2024](https://github.com/Kosthi/CSCC-DB-Rucbase-2024)** — National Collegiate Computer System Capability Challenge, database track: 2nd of 325
- **[RMDB 2023](https://github.com/Kosthi/RMDB-2023)** — the previous year's edition of the same contest: 21st of 382
- **[KDD Cup 2026](https://github.com/Kosthi/kddcup2026-dataagents)** — DataAgents track: 9th of 703
- **[ipatool-rs](https://github.com/Kosthi/ipatool-rs)** — an App Store CLI written in Rust

## About this blog

Running since 2023, originally on Hugo and moved to [Astro](https://astro.build) in 2026. The visual design is adapted from [skyzh-site](https://github.com/skyzh/skyzh-site) and [Astro Nano](https://github.com/markhorn-dev/astro-nano), both MIT licensed.

It's hosted on GitHub Pages, comments run on [Twikoo](https://twikoo.js.org/), and the source is open on [GitHub](https://github.com/Kosthi/kosthi.github.io).

Most posts exist in both Chinese and English — use the switcher in the header. A few are Chinese only.
